FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers.

Is Coinza free?

Yes. The app is free to use. An optional premium subscription is available.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional. Watching one to the end earns points.

Why didn't I get points for an ad?

Points are given only when the ad is completed. Closing it early, or reaching 5 ads that day, gives no reward.

How do I withdraw?

Open the withdrawal page in the app, enter your GCash name and number and submit. The team reviews each request.

When does the daily limit reset?

At midnight Philippine time (UTC+8).

How can I contact support?

Use the support option in the app, or email the developer.

Ready to start earning?

Coinza is coming to Google Play. Questions in the meantime? Get in touch.
