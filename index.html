FAQ

Common questions

Is Coinza free?

Yes. The app is free to use. An optional premium subscription is available.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional. Watching one to the end earns you points.

Why didn't I get points for an ad?

Points are given only when the ad is completed. Closing early, or reaching 5 ads that day, gives no reward.

How do I withdraw?

Open the withdrawal page in the app, enter your GCash name and number, and submit. The team reviews each request.

How can I contact support?

Use the support option inside the app, or email the developer.
