Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

About

Coinza is an Android rewards app developed by Sox. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name and profile photo, provided through Firebase Authentication.
  • App activity: points balance, game results, transactions, referrals and task submissions.
  • Withdrawal details: the GCash name and number you enter when you request a withdrawal.
  • Uploaded images: payment or task proof images you choose to submit.
  • Advertising data: our ad partner InMobi may collect device information, such as the advertising ID and IP address, to show ads.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your points and history
  • To review withdrawal and subscription requests
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To show rewarded ads

Services we use

Coinza uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check) to store data and InMobi to show ads. These services have their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and data by emailing us. Ads are optional, and you can use the app without watching them.

Children

Coinza is not intended for children under 13.
